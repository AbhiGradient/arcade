How to play

Keyboard: A/D or arrows move, W/Up/Space jump (hold for higher), Esc or P pause, R twice restarts.

Touch: hold the arrow buttons to run, tap JUMP. Multi-touch works.

Stomp enemies from above. Beetles need two stomps. Spikes and pits hurt.

Hit ? blocks from below for coins and mushrooms. A mushroom makes you big: you can break bricks and survive one hit. 50 coins = extra life.

Touch a checkpoint flag to respawn there. Reach the goal flag to win.
