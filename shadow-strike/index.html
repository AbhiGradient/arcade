Shadow Strike: Infinite Arena – free 2D browser fighting game

About the game

Shadow Strike: Infinite Arena is a free, single-player 2D martial-arts fighting game made with HTML5 Canvas. You play an original shadow warrior, duel one opponent at a time, earn coins and grow stronger. It is an independent project inspired by the genre and is not affiliated with any other fighting game.

How to play

Press Play and defeat the opponent by landing attacks that actually connect: every strike has a startup, an active hit window and a recovery. Block incoming hits, dodge through danger, and fill your energy bar to unleash a special attack. Win to earn coins, then spend them on weapons and upgrades before the next fight.

Desktop controls

Move with A/D or the arrow keys, jump with W or Up, hold S, Down or Space to block, punch with J, kick with K, heavy attack with L, dodge with Shift, special with E, pause with Esc.

Mobile controls

On phones and tablets, on-screen buttons appear for movement, jump, punch, kick, heavy, block, dodge, special and pause. Landscape orientation is recommended.

Weapons and combat

Choose fists, sword, dual blades, staff, spear or war hammer. Each weapon changes damage, reach and attack speed. Upgrade vitality, power, defense and focus to keep up with tougher enemies.

Endless progression

There is no final level. Every level is generated by a formula, so enemies keep gaining health, damage, speed, aggression and defense within safe limits, while new fighter types and combat modifiers combine over time. A boss appears every ten levels.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shadow Strike free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no account, ads, tracking or purchases.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing site data erases it, and you can export a backup from Settings.

Can I play offline?

Yes, once the game files are on your device. You can also open index.html directly from a downloaded folder.
